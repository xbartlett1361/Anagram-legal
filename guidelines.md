# Anagram Community Guidelines

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Anagram rooms are anonymous so people can be honest and have fun. Anonymous isn't lawless: the server knows who wrote every message, and we act on reports.

## Zero tolerance

Any of these gets content removed and the account banned, often on the first offense:

- **Sexual content involving minors**, in any form. We report it to the authorities.
- **Threats** of violence against anyone, including "jokes".
- **Harassment and bullying:** targeting someone with insults, humiliation, piling on, or repeated unwanted messages.
- **Hate:** attacking people for race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability or serious illness.
- **Sharing intimate images of someone without their consent.**
- **Doxxing:** posting someone's private information (address, phone number, private photos, accounts) without consent.
- **Encouraging self-harm or suicide.**

## Also not allowed

- Spam, scams and impersonation.
- Selling or promoting illegal goods or services.
- Trying to unmask the people behind aliases by technical means, or evading a ban.
- Graphic violence or gore.

## Keep in mind

- Everyone in a room can see the member list. Anyone with the room bubble can join, so treat a room like it could be forwarded.
- People can screenshot. Don't post what you wouldn't want seen with your name on it.
- Guesses in guess-who are just guesses. They're never confirmed; don't treat them as facts about anyone.

## What you can do

- **Report:** long-press a message → Report. You stop seeing it immediately; after three people report it, it's hidden for everyone and we review it.
- **Mute:** long-press → Mute this alias hides that symbol for you, in that room only.
- **Leave:** room menu → Leave room. Nobody is told.
- **Contact us:** [anagram.socials@gmail.com](mailto:anagram.socials@gmail.com). For emergencies, contact local emergency services first.

## What we do

We review reports, hide content, and suspend or ban accounts that break these rules. Bans apply to the account, everywhere in Anagram.
