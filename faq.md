# Anagram FAQ

**What is Anagram?**
Anonymous rooms inside your iMessage group chats. Everyone sees who's in the room; nobody sees who said what.

**How do I start a room?**
Open an iMessage group, open the app drawer (the + next to the text field), tap Anagram, then New room. Name it, pick how long it lasts, and send the bubble. Friends tap the bubble to join. Everyone needs an iPhone and the Anagram app.

**Who can see what I write?**
Everyone in the room sees your messages under a symbol, like ◆. Nobody in the room can see which symbol is yours. You get a new symbol in every room.

**Can anyone find out it was me?**
Anagram never reveals authors to other members, and guess-who results are never confirmed. To act on reports, Anagram staff can see who wrote a reported message.

**What are the limits of anonymity?**
Some things code can't fix: if you join late, people may work out your symbol from timing; your writing style can give you away; and anyone can screenshot a room.

**What happens when a room ends?**
Everything in it is deleted: messages, photos, reactions, guesses and the AI's settings. Nobody is revealed. Rooms with no end are deleted 30 days after the last member leaves.

**What does the AI character see?**
In rooms with an AI character, recent messages are sent to Anthropic to generate replies, labelled only with symbols. Names and account ids are never sent. Everyone is told before they can post in that room. Mention it with @ and its name, or @ai.

**Why does the AI run out of energy?**
Each room gets free AI credits every day (reset at 4 AM New York time). When they run out, anyone can watch a short ad to give it more, or wait until tomorrow.

**How do I report or mute someone?**
Long-press a message. Report hides it for you immediately and for everyone after three reports. Mute this alias hides that symbol for you in that room only.

**Why are photos blurred?**
So nobody sees a photo they didn't choose to. Tap to view. Location and other details are removed on your phone before upload, and there's no save button.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the Anagram app: Settings → Delete account.
