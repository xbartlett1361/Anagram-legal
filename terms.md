## 3. How anonymity works, and its limits

Inside a room, everyone sees who is a member, but messages appear under a symbol (an "alias") instead of a name. Anagram never shows other members who wrote what, and guesses made in the app are never confirmed.

Anonymity is not absolute:

- **Staff access.** To investigate reports and enforce these terms, Anagram staff can see which account wrote a message.
- **Inference.** Other people may work out who you are from timing (for example, if you join late), your writing style, or what you say.
- **Screenshots.** Anyone in a room can screenshot it. Anagram can't prevent that.
- **Legal requests.** We may disclose information when required by law (see the [Privacy Policy](privacy)).

## 4. Rooms

Any member can rename a room, add or remove its AI character, and invite others by sharing the room bubble. A room can hold up to 50 people. When a room's timer runs out, the room and everything in it (messages, photos, reactions, guesses, AI settings) are deleted, and nobody is revealed. Rooms with no end are deleted 30 days after the last member leaves.

## 7. AI characters

A room can include an AI character. When it does, recent messages in that room (under their symbols, never names) are sent to Anthropic to generate the AI's replies; every member is told before they can post there. AI replies are generated automatically and can be wrong, offensive or misleading. Don't rely on them for medical, legal, financial or safety decisions. AI features may be limited, paused or turned off at any time.
