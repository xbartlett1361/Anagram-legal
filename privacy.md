# Anagram Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 8, 2026_

Anagram is made by Xander Bartlett ("Anagram", "we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and how long we keep it. The short version: other members never learn who wrote what; we keep authorship on our servers so we can act on reports; rooms delete themselves when they end.

## What we collect

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Sign in with Apple:** an Apple account identifier and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address) | To create and secure your account |
| **Display name** (your first name from Apple, editable) | Shown in the member lists of rooms you're in. Never attached to your messages |
| **Age check result:** that you're 18+ and when you confirmed it. Your birthdate is used once for the check and **not stored** | Anagram is for adults only |
| **Terms acceptance time, theme, notification settings** | To run the app the way you set it up |
| **Push token** for this iPhone | To send you notifications |
| **Rooms you're in and your alias in each** | To show your rooms and keep your symbol consistent |
| **What you post:** messages, photos (with location and other metadata removed on your phone before upload), replies, reactions, poll votes | To show them to the room |
| **Who wrote each message**, stored separately from the message and never sent to other members | To investigate reports and enforce our rules |
| **Guesses, reports, mutes, AI notice acceptance** | Guess-who, moderation and the AI notice. Other members only ever see totals |
| **AI usage:** per room, the model used and the number of tokens (no message text) | To control AI costs |

We don't collect your contacts, location, or the content of your iMessage conversations. Anagram only sees what is posted inside Anagram rooms.

The app keeps a short log of its last 20 errors on your phone to help with troubleshooting. It stays on your phone.

## Who sees what

- **Other members of a room** see your display name in the member list, and your messages under your alias, never linked to each other by Anagram. They see reactions, guesses and poll votes only as counts.
- **Anagram staff** can see who wrote a message when investigating a report or enforcing the [Terms](terms). Access is limited to that purpose.
- **Nobody** gets your messages or account data for marketing. We don't sell personal information.

## Service providers

| Provider | What they process | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase** (hosting, United States) | All of the account and room data above | Database, storage, realtime delivery and server functions |
| **Anthropic** | Only in rooms with an AI character: the room's recent messages (up to 40), labelled with symbols like ◆, plus the AI's name, personality and the room context members wrote. **Never names, emails or account ids.** Photos appear only as "[photo]" | To generate the AI character's replies. Every member is told before they can post in such a room |
| **Google AdMob** | Device and ad-interaction data, IP address, and your advertising identifier **only if you allow tracking**. For rewarded ads, the room id (no account id) to confirm the reward | To show ads and pay for the free app |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple; push notification delivery | Sign-in and notifications. Notifications show a symbol and message text, never a name |

## Tracking

The first time ads load in the app, Anagram asks whether you allow tracking. If you decline, ads still show but aren't personalized with your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

## How long we keep it

- **Rooms:** when a room's timer ends, the room and everything in it (messages, photos, authorship records, reactions, guesses, reports, mutes, poll votes, AI settings) are deleted within minutes. Rooms with no end are deleted 30 days after the last member leaves.
- **Your account:** until you delete it. **Settings → Delete account** removes your profile, memberships, aliases, guesses, votes, devices, and the records linking you to messages you wrote. Messages you already sent stay, anonymous, in their rooms until those rooms end.
- **AI usage counts** (no content) are kept for cost reporting.
- Deleted data may remain in our providers' backups for a limited period before it is overwritten.

## Legal requests and safety

We may preserve or disclose information, including authorship of a message, if we believe in good faith it's required by law or necessary to prevent serious harm. We report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

## Your choices and rights

You can edit your display name, theme and notification settings in the app, and delete your account in Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to some processing. Email us to make a request; we'll respond within the time the law requires.

## Children

Anagram is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if we learn we have, we delete the account.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we'll tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
